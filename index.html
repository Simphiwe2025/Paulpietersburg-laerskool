About Laerskool Paulpietersburg

Our History

Paulpietersburg Primary School was established in April 1894.

It has had 17 principals to date. Our first principal was Mr. H.W. Stumpf. Our current principal, Mr. V.D. Eva, has been our principal since August 1997.

Our Badge

Our school badge was designed by Mr T. Meyer. The colors of the badge are gold and orange. The background is blue.

The joining of the three schools are reflected in the badge as:
~ The aloe from Paulpietersburg School.
~ The lion from Braunschweig School.
~ The book of knowledge and the Bible from Commondale School.

The motto of the school is "Scientia est Potestas" which means "Knowledge is power".

Our Mission

Provide quality education by means of instruction, guidance and facilitation by a core of dedicated and accountable educators. Raise standards for learners at all levels both in and out of school. Encourage parental and community involvement in our school. The school will be managed in a Christian environment.

Our Vision

Paulpietersburg Primary School Strives to provide quality education to learners in order to uplift and empower them to play a responsible and meaningful role in society.
